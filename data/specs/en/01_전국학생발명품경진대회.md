# Overview

This invention project addressed hygiene and portability problems that arise when carrying a toothbrush. I analyzed moisture, bacterial growth, and inconvenient storage in conventional cases, then developed a ventilated and compact structure.

## My Contribution

- Defined the problem and planned the invention
- Designed the toothbrush storage structure
- Developed use scenarios and improvement directions
- Prepared the invention presentation materials

## Outcome

- Entered the National Student Science Invention Contest
- Received the Minister of Environment Award
